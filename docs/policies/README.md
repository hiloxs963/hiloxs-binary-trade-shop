# Policy Draft Placeholders

The following customer-facing policies are required before public launch but are **pending final
owner/legal approval** and are not published by Phase 11:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Delivery Policy
- Returns and Refund Policy
- Contact and Support Policy
- Marketplace and Seller Terms

Approved drafts must use verified business identity/contact details and define actual data purposes,
retention, delivery scope, cancellation/refund handling, marketplace responsibilities, dispute path,
and governing terms. Do not invent a registered address, legal entity, telephone, delivery SLA,
refund timeframe, seller verification claim, or governing law. Publishing and linking final policy
routes remains a launch blocker.
